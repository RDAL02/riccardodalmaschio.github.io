What I do

At Click and Find I lead and develop a forecasting system for petroleum product prices based on Platts and ICE benchmarks. The forecasts support speculation and hedging in logistics and procurement, via futures and swaps, and are built on deep learning, transformers and LLM-derived text embeddings.

Before that, at Cluster Reply I developed on AX 2012 and Dynamics 365 for Finance and Operations, writing and maintaining X++ customisations and designing a multi-agent pipeline for automated purchase accrual reconciliation in D365.

Alongside that, my research work has focused on applying language models and text embeddings to financial forecasting, which led to my Master's thesis on commodity price prediction and two conference publications.
