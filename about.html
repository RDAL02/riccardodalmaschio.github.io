About

A bit more about me

Background

I'm a computer engineer based in Padua, Italy, working as a consultant at Cluster Reply on Microsoft ERP platforms.

Day to day I develop on AX 2012 and Dynamics 365 for Finance and Operations, writing and maintaining X++ customisations and designing solutions around the business flows they support. One of the projects I'm proudest of is a multi-agent pipeline for automated purchase accrual reconciliation in D365.

I graduated in July 2026 with a Master's degree in Computer Engineering from the University of Padua (110 cum laude), following a Bachelor's in Information Engineering at the same university (110 cum laude). My Master's thesis looked at commodity price forecasting using time-series returns augmented with dense news-derived features, a project that pulled me deep into quantitative finance, price assessments and text embeddings. I also spent the start of 2024 on Erasmus at the University of California Santa Cruz, studying computer networks and wireless communications.

Before consulting, I worked alongside an industrial engineer building an algorithm that ran on Ewon switches to collect, process and transmit plant data to customer servers, including emergency and failure detection with automated alerts. Earlier still, I produced video and promotional photography, and spent several years tutoring and giving private lessons.

Objectives

In the short term I aim to advance my career through hands-on experience, continuous learning and increasing responsibility, working toward managerial roles. Looking further ahead, I'm eager to use that experience to build and grow my own business.

Interests

I'm generally curious, and drawn to deepening my knowledge of topics I know little or nothing about. In particular: technology, engineering, finance, economics, philosophy and politics.
