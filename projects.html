MSc thesis · 2026

Can the news predict oil prices?

I tested whether the news helps forecast Brent crude oil prices. From news articles I extracted two kinds of features: sentiment and sentence embeddings. I fed them to MLP and LSTM models, together with past prices. I also tested a realistic setting, where the models only see information available at the time. Embeddings worked better than sentiment. News and prices together gave the best results.

  • Time series
  • NLP
  • Text embeddings
  • Deep learning

Click and Find · 2026 – now

Petroleum price forecasting

Builds on my MSc thesis

I manage and develop a project to build a system that forecasts petroleum product prices, taking the research of my thesis further. The forecaster combines Platts and ICE price benchmarks with news. Its forecasts have two uses: in the wholesale business, to hedge with futures and swaps and to choose the best time to buy; and in trading, to speculate on price moves.

BSc thesis · 2024

Interconnected lightsails for space exploration

A swarm of lightsail probes for space exploration, linked by laser communication. I compared modulation schemes and used link budget calculations to find the maximum distance between probes, then proposed a line topology in which data hops from the furthest probe to the closest, with redundancy in case some probes fail.
